The problem

Studying on a phone, with no data left

Course material reaches a student as WhatsApp PDFs, photographed notes and slide decks. Three things break the reading.

Unknown words stop you

Leaving the page to search a term costs attention and mobile data, and there is often no data at all.

Scanned notes are just pictures

They cannot be searched, selected, copied or read aloud, which shuts out anyone who studies by listening.

Every subject, a different format

Slides are .pptx, assignments are .docx, textbooks are .pdf. Converting a deck to text destroys the diagrams.

Features

Everything in the build

Five formats, one library

PDF, EPUB, TXT, PPTX and DOCX, with covers, progress and resume where you stopped.

Original slide design

PowerPoint and Word open with real shape positions, theme colours, fonts, pictures and tables, pinch zoomable like a PDF page.

Tap for a meaning

Works on the PDF page itself. Phrases, and selection down to a single letter.

Word tracking read aloud

The spoken word is highlighted and the page scrolls with the voice, in portrait and zoomed landscape.

Offline text recognition

Scanned pages become selectable, searchable, speakable text without a single network call.

AI summary that reads properly

Equations come back as V₂O₅ and 1.06 F g⁻¹, not raw LaTeX.

Highlights you keep

Saved as .txt files with book name and page, and exportable as a formatted PDF.

Difficult word lists

Per book and a master list, built from what you actually looked up.

Statistics and streaks

Minutes read per day, a seven day chart and a daily streak.

Backup and restore

One zip carries books, progress, highlights, notes and settings to a new phone.

Reading comfort

Light, sepia, lavender and dark themes, auto scroll at real reading speeds, immersive full page mode.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no advertising. Books stay on the phone.

Install

Three steps

  1. Download the APK with the button at the top of this page.
  2. Allow installing from unknown sources when Android asks.
  3. Grant storage access, then copy books into Aali Reader/Books or import them from the library screen.

Optional: paste a free Google Gemini key in Settings to switch AI summaries on. If the main APK will not install, use the universal one.

Build from source

Two commands

git clone https://github.com/JH-Aali-7/aalis-reader.git
cd aalis-reader
./gradlew assembleRelease

Kotlin, Gradle 8.7, AGP 8.5.2, minSdk 24. Open the folder in Android Studio and press Run for day to day work.
